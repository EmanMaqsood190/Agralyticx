Agralyticx is a role-based agricultural ecosystem bringing farmers, transport agents, students, researchers, and investors onto one platform. Its proposed architecture combines a JavaScript frontend, Flask backend, MongoDB data layer, and an AI integration layer, with the backend serving as the central bridge between users, data, and AI services. At the heart of the farmer experience is AI Saathi, an agricultural AI companion built around SEE → TAP → HEAR → ACT, enabling accessible voice, text, and image-based interaction without requiring advanced digital literacy. Beyond the farm, Agralyticx aims to connect logistics, research, collaboration, industry, and investment turning a farmer's problem into a research opportunity, a research opportunity into an industry solution, and ultimately bringing value back to the field.
